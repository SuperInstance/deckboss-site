What deckboss does

Species identification

Deck cameras watch your crew sort into totes—port means pink, center means chum. The crew's sorting becomes ground truth. No annotation contractors. No labeling overhead.

Underwater to surface

When a fish leaves the underwater frame and breaks surface seconds later, timestamps match it back. Your underwater footage gets labeled automatically. No extra sensors needed in the column.

Sounder self-trains

Count the hooks on your vertical gear—30 hooks, 1.5 fathoms apart—and the echogram gets depth labels without the underwater camera. Your sounder model improves every night.

Overnight autotrain

While the boat sits idle at night, the day's labeled data retrains the model. Next day, the new version runs live. A/B tested against your crew's continued sorting. Rollback is the default.

Weight records

Hang the scale in view of a camera. Your crew weighs fish the same way—the camera keeps records nobody keeps. Passively. When speed isn't a factor.

Best-shot audit

One best-shot frame per fish, essential frames for review. You flip through pictures from the wheelhouse when identity matters—during openings where only pinks and chums are legal.

    Honest about what can go wrong

    Mis-sorted fish

    A pink in the center hold teaches the system chum. Your captain's best-shot audit quarantines suspicious labels before they retrain the model. Identity-critical decisions only.

    Overlap windows

    When fishing is hot, two fish can transit the underwater-to-surface window at once. We flag ambiguous matches, never guess. A lost label costs one example. A wrong label costs trust.

    Night-retrain drift

    Self-labeling can feedback into itself and degrade slowly. The A/B cell gates every promotion: if tonight's model doesn't beat tomorrow's tote labels, it doesn't ship. Rollback is automatic.

    Infrastructure that scales with your crew

  • Temporary training rig

    Global-shutter stereo cameras bootstrap the model. Once the system works on cheap permanent cameras—tote rule as ground truth—the training rig gets removed and becomes a rental for technician training and new deployments.

  • No babysitting

    The improvement loop is scheduled: night cron, next-day A/B, repeat. Not a habit. Not an agent. A tick in the substrate that runs whether anyone's watching or not.

  • Grounded in quilt

    Built on quilt-verilog: cells, links, dials, ticks. The pipeline is wiring, not programming. A deckhand who understands totes can audit any cell. Everything is data: rules, tables, schedules, gates, ledgers.
