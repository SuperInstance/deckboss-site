Frequently asked

What's included in the pricing?
Hardware installation, camera calibration, cell configuration for your boat's layout, and ongoing model updates. Everything you need to run independent of us once it's deployed.
Do you charge per fish or per day?
No. We charge once for deployment and model setup. Monthly updates are included. The system runs on your boat's network; you own the data and the trained models.
What about the training rig?
The expensive stereo cameras are rented temporarily—typically one season. Once the model works on your cheap permanent cameras (via the tote rule), the rig comes off and goes to the next boat or your training program. You own the weights; the hardware depreciates into the rental business.
How long to deploy?
Install → calibrate → run initial tote-label loop → audit captain's review → first retrain. Typically 2-4 weeks of active fishing to bootstrap. A/B promotion starts day one; the system improves nightly.
What if we want to expand to more boats later?
The system is built for multi-vessel deployment from the start. Models are portable; the trained weights move between boats. Adding a second vessel is an installation and calibration—not a new license.
Do you keep the data?
No. The labeled data (tote labels, weights, timestamps) stays on your boat. You can export it anytime. The trained models are yours to keep or update on your own timeline.
What about support when the model misbehaves?
Your captain's best-shot audit is the first gate—that catches and quarantines bad labels. If the A/B cell flags drift, rollback happens automatically. We monitor overnight retrains and help tune the system once it's live. But the system is built to be stable without constant human oversight.
