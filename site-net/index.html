Training rig rental

Global-shutter stereo cameras for bootstrap training, removed once your model is production-ready.

What's included

Cameras
Global-shutter stereo pair, 1920×1200, 90 fps, IP67 rated. Temporary deployment only.
Calibration data
Factory intrinsic and relative pose provided. On-site calibration during deployment course included.
Supporting hardware
Underwater housing, brackets, cables, adapters for your boat's configuration. Return packaging included.
Technical support
Email + phone support during rental period. Remote diagnostics, firmware updates, cable/housing repairs.
Lifespan on your boat
Typically one full season (~6-12 months). Removed once your system works on cheap permanent cameras and the tote rule is calibrated.
Pricing: Seasonal or annual rental rates depend on deployment timeline and your vessel class. Ask about multi-boat fleet discounts. Damage outside normal operation may incur repair fees.

Return process

1. You schedule removal with us when the model is stable on permanent cameras. 2. We coordinate logistics and ship return boxes. 3. You disconnect, pack, and ship the rig back. 4. We inspect, recalibrate, and make ready for the next deployment. 5. Your rental period ends.

How it works economically

The stereo rig is scaffolding, not product. It bootstraps your depth labels and trains the sounder. Once the system learns from the tote rule and hook counting, cheap permanent cameras are all you need. The trained model is portable; the equipment depreciates into a rental business that funds new deployments and training courses. You buy the weights; you rent the hardware that produced them.
